Privacy Policy

Scroll Interrupts Studio · Effective October 15, 2026

Scroll Interrupts Studio ("the app") is a personal tool the owner of the Scroll Interrupts YouTube channel uses to upload short videos to that channel. This policy explains what the app accesses and what it does with it.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for a single permission: to upload videos to your YouTube channel (the youtube.upload scope). It does not read your existing videos, comments, subscribers, analytics, email address or any other data in your Google account.

What the app stores

  • The sign-in token Google issues, stored in a file on the computer where the app runs.
  • The video files it creates, their titles and descriptions, and a log of uploads (YouTube video IDs and scheduled publish times), stored on the same computer.

The app has no servers or databases, and it uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. Nothing it stores leaves the computer it runs on, except the videos and their details, which are sent to YouTube when they are uploaded.

How the information is used and shared

The sign-in token is used only to upload and schedule videos on the signed-in channel. The app does not sell, rent or share any information with anyone. The only data it sends anywhere goes to Google, through the YouTube Data API, to carry out uploads.

Keeping or removing access

The sign-in token is kept until you remove it. You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google security settings. Deleting the token file on the computer also removes the app's access.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and sets no cookies. GitHub may collect technical information such as IP addresses, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

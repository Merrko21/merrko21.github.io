Privacy Policy

Scroll Interrupts Studio · Effective October 19, 2026

Scroll Interrupts Studio ("the app") is a personal tool the owner of the Scroll Interrupts YouTube channel uses to upload short videos to that channel and to post to Facebook Pages the same person manages. No one else signs in to it. This policy explains what the app accesses and what it does with it.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for a single permission: to upload videos to your YouTube channel (the youtube.upload scope). It does not read your existing videos, comments, subscribers, analytics, email address or any other data in your Google account.

On Facebook, the app uses Meta's Graph API with the pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement and pages_manage_posts permissions, only to list the Pages its owner manages and to publish or schedule Reels and posts on those Pages. It does not read anyone's profile, friends, messages or other personal data.

What the app stores

  • The sign-in token Google issues and the Facebook Page access tokens, stored in files on the computer where the app runs.
  • The video files it creates, their titles and descriptions, the text of its posts, and a log of uploads and posts (YouTube and Facebook IDs and scheduled publish times), stored on the same computer.

The app has no servers or databases, and it uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. Nothing it stores leaves the computer it runs on, except the videos, posts and their details, which are sent to YouTube and Facebook when they are published.

How the information is used and shared

The tokens are used only to upload, post and schedule content on the owner's own YouTube channel and Facebook Pages. The app does not sell, rent or share any information with anyone. The only data it sends anywhere goes to Google, through the YouTube Data API, and to Meta, through the Graph API, to carry out uploads and posts.

Keeping or removing access

The sign-in token is kept until you remove it. You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google security settings. Deleting the token file on the computer also removes the app's access.

Deleting your data

The app keeps no data about anyone other than its owner, and all of it is on the owner's computer. To remove the app's access to Facebook, go to Facebook Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations and remove Scroll Interrupts Studio. To have anything the app stored deleted, email mrakovich21@gmail.com; it is deleted within 30 days. Content already published to YouTube or Facebook can be deleted there at any time.

Scroll Interrupts Studio

The publishing tool behind the Scroll Interrupts YouTube channel.

Scroll Interrupts Studio finds the most replayed moments of long-form videos, turns them into vertical clips with captions, and uploads them to the Scroll Interrupts channel on a posting schedule using the YouTube Data API.

This is a personal tool, not a public service. It runs on the channel owner's own computer, and the channel owner is the only person who signs in to it. It only asks Google for permission to upload videos to the signed-in channel.
